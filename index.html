How to Play Hexxagon

Hexxagon is a tactical board game where two or three forces battle to infect and conquer the hexagonal arena!

DISTANCE 1: CLONE

Click an adjacent green hex to multiply (+1 piece). Your original piece stays.

DISTANCE 2: JUMP

Click a yellow hex 2 spaces away to teleport. Moves original piece.

⚡ Infection & Capture

Whenever you land on any hex, all adjacent enemy pieces are immediately converted to your color!

🏆 Winning

When the board is filled or no moves remain, the player with the most pieces wins!
